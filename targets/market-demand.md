# Market demand — GENERATED, safe to delete

This file is written by research runs, not by hand. It records how often each tool appears in current
postings, so `/find-jobs` can rank a stack honestly and the resume can lead with what employers
actually ask for.

Regenerate it by asking the agent to survey current postings. The shape it produces:

| 3 | ETL / ELT | 145 | 42.2% | yes |

Counting unit is the posting: how many postings mention the item at least once, not how many times
it appears. Record the corpus size, the date and the sources, or the numbers mean nothing later.
